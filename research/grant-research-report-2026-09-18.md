# Grants & Funding Research Report — Black AI Tech Services Founder (Saint Louis / National / Waukegan-Lake County)
**As of: September 18, 2026** | Business profile: Black man running a small AI technology services company (AI consulting, app development, data engineering)

## Executive Summary

**Honest headline: direct cash grants that are open *right now* for a Black male AI-services founder are limited.** The strongest currently-open options are: (1) the City of Waukegan Microenterprise Grant (up to $25,000, first-come first-served, application live — Waukegan-located businesses only); (2) the Illinois DCEO SBIR/STTR Phase I match (up to ~$75,000, rolling, but only for firms with a federal SBIR/STTR Phase I award doing qualifying R&D); and (3) race-neutral national micro-grants that are genuinely accepting applications now (NASE up to $4,000 quarterly; Freed Fellowship $500/month). The marquee Black-founder programs are mostly in their 2026 off-cycle: NAACP Powershift ($25k), Black Ambition (up to $1M), Braze Tech for an Equitable Future, and Comcast RISE (2026 closed; St. Louis and Lake County were not eligible markets). Fearless Fund's Strivers grant is permanently ended. In Saint Louis, the headline programs are not grants at all: MTC's IDEA Fund is equity co-investment (Software & Data is a focus area — strong conditional fit; October 2026 cycle just closed, January 2027 cycle next), and SLDC's ScaleUpSTL is low-interest loans. Arch Grants ($75k + $25k) is the best future grant fit but closed February–March 2026; it returns ~February 2027.

**Five strongest fits per angle are flagged below** with status (OPEN NOW / WATCH / CLOSED / NOT A GRANT / NOT ELIGIBLE). Where five genuinely-strong options did not exist, that is stated rather than padded.
