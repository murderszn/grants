# WOC Grant Research — September 18, 2026

Ranked shortlist of small-business grants for women of color, researched for
**Sierhah Price's** run. Applications go in her name, not Joshua's. Nothing
has been applied for or submitted.

## 1. HerRise MicroGrant — HerSuiteSpot / Yva Jourdan Foundation
- Amount: $1,000/month
- Deadline: last day of each month, 11:59 PM ET (next: Sept 30, 2026)
- Eligibility: 51% women-owned, US-registered, <$1M gross revenue; for-profit
  only (excludes nonprofits, franchises, direct sellers, resellers,
  independent consultants); explicitly focused on women of color
- Fee: $15 nonrefundable (confirmed in their FAQ)
- Apply: https://www.hersuitespot.com/herrise/
- Notes: running since 2017 with published winner lists. Same operator as the
  Hustler's MicroGrant Joshua skipped for himself.

## 2. Amber Grant — WomensNet
- Amount: three $10,000 grants each month (general / startup / rotating
  category) + three $50,000 year-end grants
- Deadline: monthly, 1st–last day of month, 11:59 PM ET (current cycle ends
  Sept 30, 2026)
- Eligibility: women 18+ owning ≥50% of a US/Canada business; idea-stage and
  pre-revenue welcome
- Fee: $15 nonrefundable (hardship waiver: info@ambergrantsforwomen.com)
- Rules: https://ambergrantsforwomen.com/grant-rules/ (amounts, cadence, fee
  confirmed on official rules page, Sept 18, 2026)
- Notes: September's rotating category is Education & Child Care; the general
  monthly grant has no industry restriction.

## 3. H&R Block Fund Her Future (via Hello Alice) — URGENT
- Amount: up to four $50,000 packages each ($45,000 cash + $5,000 H&R Block
  services)
- Deadline: opened Sept 1, 2026 — **closes Sept 22, 2026 at 6 PM ET**
- Eligibility: founder/CEO-level woman 18+; for-profit business registered in
  any US state, DC, or territory
- Fee: none listed
- Listing: https://www.helloalice.com/opportunities/grants/h-r-block-fund-her-future-grant-2026
- Notes: biggest dollar, no fee, but needs a same-day decision. Requires a
  registered for-profit US business — confirm which venture applies.

## 4. Women Founders Grant
- Amount: $5,000/month
- Deadline: most listings say last day of each month; US Chamber (updated
  ~Sept 2026) lists Oct 31, 2026 — confirm exact current cycle before applying
- Eligibility: women 18+, US-based, business idea or existing business ≥51%
  women-owned/operated; idea-stage welcome, no LLC/website required
- Fee: $25 nonrefundable; application is just two questions
- FAQ: https://womenfoundersgrant.com/gimme-women-owned-business-grants/faqs

## 5. IFundWomen Universal Grant Application
- Amount: varies — free marketplace matching women-owned businesses to
  corporate grants (Visa, Amex, Neutrogena, Johnnie Walker, etc.)
- Deadline: rolling — apply once, get notified of matches
- Eligibility: women-owned businesses of any stage or industry
- Fee: none for the universal application
- Pages: https://www.ifundwomen.com/grants/universal-grant-application-0 ·
  https://www.ifundwomen.com/grants/apply-for-grants
- Notes: zero-cost, one application, multiple shots; good baseline play.

## 6. Freed Fellowship Grant
- Amount: $500/month + $2,500 year-end grant for monthly winners
- Deadline: last day of each month, midnight ET
- Eligibility: any US micro/small business owner; women and minorities
  explicitly encouraged (not women-exclusive); nonprofits excluded
- Fee: $19 (includes 5C-framework business feedback + 2-month Freed Studio
  trial even if not selected)
- Page: https://freedfellowship.com (fee, amounts, cadence confirmed on
  official FAQ, Sept 18, 2026)

## 7. EmpowHer Grants — Boundless Futures Foundation (opens soon)

- Eligibility: women founders 22+, US, early-stage business (up to 5 years)

- Fee: none listed
- Site: boundlessfutures.org
- Notes: largest no-fee option; requires a social-impact angle — calendar
