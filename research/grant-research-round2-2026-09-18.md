# Grants research — Round 2: NEW opportunities for Black-owned AI tech services company (Delaware entity, Waukegan IL / St. Louis MO)
Mission date: Friday, September 18, 2026. None of the Round-1 programs are repeated here.

## Summary

All findings below are NEW (not in the exclusion list). The biggest honest headline: several high-profile Black-founder and corporate programs from past years are closed, paused, or retired in 2026 (Google Black Founders Fund, Comcast RISE, FedEx grants, many Illinois DCEO grant lines). The strongest **open-now** fits for this founder are general/non-targeted grants with rolling or imminent deadlines, plus one major federal R&D grant and one accelerator deadline days away.

### The 5 strongest new fits overall

1. **Verizon Small Business Digital Ready — $10,000 grant, OPEN NOW, rolling monthly.** Official page (digitalready.verizonwireless.com/funding) shows applications open; 10 x $10,000 awarded monthly June–December 2026. Free entry: register, complete any two free courses/events in 2026 to unlock the application, submit once — it stays eligible all year. Details in `notes/verizon-digital-ready.md`.
2. **Skip Instant Grants — up to $1,000, WEEKLY live drawings.** Ongoing Tuesdays/Fridays 3PM ET drawings (official help page). Answer a few questions, attend the YouTube livestream, claim within ~60 seconds. Free; win once per 4 months. Lowest effort-to-cash route available. Details in `notes/skip-instant-grants.md`.
3. **Business Freedom Grant — $1,000, URGENT: early deadline September 30, 2026 (12 days).** Free application open to any small business; scored on a 10-point rubric; winners announced on YouTube. Later deadlines Nov 15 and Dec 31, 2026. Details in `notes/business-freedom-grant.md`.
4. **NSF America's Seed Fund — up to ~$2,000,000, non-dilutive, OPEN NOW (rolling Project Pitch).** Federal GRANT (zero equity). Phase I up to $305,000; AI is an explicitly eligible field. Only a strong fit if the company frames a genuine deep-tech R&D effort (e.g., proprietary computer vision), not routine consulting/app work. Details in `notes/nsf-seed-fund.md`.
5. **Secretsos Small Business Grant — up to $2,500, closes September 30, 2026, quarterly.** Simple quarterly grant for US small businesses (per weekly-verified directory; direct page fetch failed — confirm on helloskip-owned Secretsos page before applying). Details in `notes/ueni-grant-directory.md`.
