# Note: NSF America's Seed Fund (SBIR/STTR)
URLs:
- https://www.NSF.gov/science-matters/nsf-101-americas-seed-fund (official)
- https://seedfund.nsf.gov/ (official portal)
Read: 2026-09-18 via browser_search/index.
Facts:
- Non-dilutive federal R&D funding (GRANT): up to ~$2M total (Phase I up to $305,000 for 6–18 months; Phase II up to $1,250,000 over ~24 months; Phase IIB supplements possible).
- Zero equity taken; company keeps control and IP.
- Rolling: 3-page Project Pitch can be submitted anytime; if invited, full Phase I proposal; ~1-month pitch decision, ~6-month proposal decision. Three annual proposal review deadlines.
- Eligibility: US for-profit small business <500 employees, ≥50% US citizen/PR owned; all work in US; PI employed by company.
- Funds ~400 startups/year; AI explicitly in portfolio. Does NOT fund "straightforward engineering or incremental product development" — needs deep-tech/R&D angle (computer vision R&D could fit; pure services/consulting work is a weak fit).
- FY2024: 255 Phase I awards; 96% had <10 employees.
Angle: 2 (grant, federal innovation). Mission asked for "beyond SBIR (e.g., NSF, DOE...)"; this IS NSF's SBIR/STTR program but it is the flagship federal non-dilutive AI-startup route and rolling — include with the caveat. No DOE non-SBIR AI grant for services companies found.
