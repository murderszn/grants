Facts (all from Verizon's own page unless noted):
- $10,000 national grants; 10 selected per month, June–December 2026 (LISC says 70 total in 2026).
- Apply once in 2026 and remain eligible for grants awarded throughout the year; unselected applications roll forward monthly. Final decisions by Jan 12, 2027.
- Eligibility: owners of for-profit US small businesses (incl. Puerto Rico, USVI), 18+.
- How to apply: register on Digital Ready, complete any combination of two free courses or events in 2026 to unlock the grant application; then submit one application.
- Grant (non-repayable); Form 1099 issued — taxable income.
- Since 2021: $15M awarded to ~1,500 businesses.
- Partnership with LISC (nonprofit administrator).
