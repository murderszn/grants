Read: 2026-09-18 via browser_search/index.
Facts:
- New in 2026: interest forms opened March 20, 2026; program began April 27, 2026.
- Fund: $20,000 (National League of Cities grant), administered via Evanston Community Foundation; tiers incl. up to $500 (Tier 1 concept/skill development).
- Eligibility: Black residents of Evanston owning or launching a business within Evanston city limits; mandatory SBDC (Oakton College) onboarding; no prior committee funding.
- VERDICT: NOT eligible — founder is in Waukegan (Lake County), not Evanston. Included only to document the check.
