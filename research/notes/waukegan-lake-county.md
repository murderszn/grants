# Angle C — Waukegan / Lake County, IL (Sept 18, 2026 findings)

## City of Waukegan Microenterprise Small Business Grant Program — VERIFIED LIVE (Sept 18, 2026)
- Official page: https://www.waukeganil.gov/942 — online application currently live.
- 2026 instructions (PDF fetched live): https://www.waukeganil.gov/DocumentCenter/View/11084/2026-Microenterprise-Small-Business-Grant-Program---Application-Instructions
- Award: up to $25,000 per business; one-time only; first-come, first-served until funds expended.
- Eligibility:
  - Active business physically located in City of Waukegan; brick-and-mortar (food trucks and online businesses excluded; home-based may qualify with property details).
  - ≤5 employees (independently/family-owned); operating 24 consecutive months.
  - Entity: sole prop, partnership, S-corp, corporation, LLC/LLP.
  - Net annual business income <$300,000 (2024 & 2025 federal returns).
  - Owner/employees meet HUD low-to-moderate income thresholds (e.g., 1-person household ≤$68,050; 2 ≤$77,800; 3 ≤$80,750; 4 ≤$97,200).
  - Must create/retain ≥1 permanent full-time job; technical assistance (budgeting/marketing/business planning) required; UEI# + SAM.gov registration; wet-signature of forms in person at CDBG office (100 N. MLK Jr. Ave, 3rd Floor, Waukegan City Hall).
  - NOT eligible: businesses that received any CDBG or COVID funding assistance.
  - No duplication of benefits with other CARES Act/PPP/CDBG sources.
- Use of funds: payroll, rent/lease, utilities, inventory, technology capabilities, working capital.
- Contact: (847) 599-2530; CDBG@waukeganil.gov.

## Illinois DCEO SBIR/STTR Phase I Matching Program (index)
- Official: https://dceo.illinois.gov/whyillinois/sbir-sttr-phase1.html
- Non-competitive, first-come first-served. Matches 75% of federal SBIR/STTR Phase I award: up to $56,250 initially + remaining 25% (typically $18,750) after milestones → up to ~$75,000 total.
- Requires: for-profit business with principal place of business in Illinois + recent active federal SBIR/STTR Phase I award.
- Strong conditional fit: excellent for AI company doing qualifying R&D with/winning a federal SBIR award; ordinary consulting work alone does not qualify. Phase II matching details not verified — check DCEO page.
- New (Sept 2026): DCEO grant-portal pages reorganized as part of Illinois' 10-year economic growth plan — watch for updates. https://dceo.illinois.gov/news/2026/featured-story/news-34385.html
