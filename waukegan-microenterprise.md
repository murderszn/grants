# Waukegan Microenterprise Small Business Grant — Application Guide

Companion to [issue #1](https://github.com/murderszn/grants/issues/1). Up to **$25,000**, one-time, first-come first-served.

## The one thing that matters most

Your place in line is set when the **complete** package is received: online application + **all** documents + wet signatures. An incomplete submission just sits in "pending." Completeness = speed.

## Eligibility (confirmed)

- Active business physically located in the City of Waukegan (home-based OK with property details)
- 5 or fewer employees, independently/family-owned
- Open and operating 24 consecutive months
- Sole prop, partnership, S-corp, corporation, LLC/LLP
- Net annual business income under $300,000 (2024 + 2025 federal returns)
- Owner/employees meet HUD low-to-moderate income limits (1-person household ≤ $68,050; 2 ≤ $77,800; 3 ≤ $80,750; 4 ≤ $97,200; 5 ≤ $105,000; 6 ≤ $112,800)
- Must create or retain at least 1 permanent full-time job
- Must complete technical assistance (budgeting, marketing, or business planning) alongside the grant
- Must obtain UEI # and register on SAM.gov (you may apply while registration is processing)
- Must NOT have received any CDBG or COVID relief funding (including PPP)

## How the money can be used

Payroll (retention/new hire), rent/lease, utilities, inventory, technology capabilities, working capital. Cannot duplicate expenses already paid by another funding source.

- [ ] 2024 + 2025 bank statements

## Steps

1. **Register on SAM.gov for a UEI #** — free, takes time to process. Start immediately: https://sam.gov
2. **Gather every document above** — submit everything at once; partial = pending.
3. **Complete the online application** at https://www.waukeganil.gov/942 ("Online Application" link). Only ONE person per business may apply.
4. **Go in person** to the CDBG office — 100 N Martin Luther King Jr. Ave, 3rd Floor, Waukegan City Hall — to wet-sign the "Authority to Verify Credit Information" and "Certification and Assurance" forms. The application is NOT complete until this is done.
5. **Wait for verification** — staff verify income, employees, insurance, lease/title, duplication of benefits, etc. Takes 5–6 weeks. They may email/call for clarification — check spam folders.
6. **If awarded** — you'll get an email/letter with the award amount and estimated payment date. Then: quarterly reports + P&L statements + receipts for eligible expenses.

## Disqualifiers to avoid

- Missing documents or incomplete answers
- Business not in Waukegan / operating under 24 months / more than 5 employees
- Food truck or purely online business
- Same expenses already reimbursed by another source (duplication of benefits)
- Not signing in person at the CDBG office
- A second person/partner from the same business also applying
