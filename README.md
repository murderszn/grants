## Verified skip list (don't waste time)

These were researched Sept 2026 and are either ineligible or dead:

- **Amber Grant** — requires 50%+ women ownership
- **Fearless Fund Strivers** — permanently ended (2024 settlement)
- **digitalundivided / IFundWomen** — women-only
- **SoGal Black Founder Startup Grant** — Black women/nonbinary only
- **HerRise MicroGrant** — women of color only
- **Money Moves Summit pitch** ($10k pool) — Black women only
- **Village Capital 2026 US** — women-led only
- **Backstage Capital** — VC, not a grant
- **Comcast RISE** — 2026 round closed; St. Louis and Waukegan weren't eligible markets
- **Google Black Founders Fund** — no 2026 US cycle (paused)
- **SLDC ScaleUpSTL** — low-interest loans, not grants
- **Toast / Faire / Jobber / Santander grants** — restricted to their industries
- **Wish Local Empowerment** ($500–$2,000) — brick-and-mortar storefronts only
- **DoorDash Restaurant Disaster Relief** ($10,000) — restaurant-only
- **Evanston Reparations Black Business Grant** — Evanston residents only (Waukegan is out)
- **Braze Tech for an Equitable Future** — 2026 closed Jan 16
- **Galaxy of Stars Galaxy Grants** ($3,500) — April 2026 round closed; periodic, low priority
- **BALSA Grant** ($1,500, STL first-time founders) — 2026 deadline was July 27
- **MTC MOBEC** — for entrepreneur-support organizations only, not businesses
- **Illinois DCEO Small Business Capital and Infrastructure Grant** — discontinued
- **JPMorgan Chase Diverse Supplier Grant** — 2022-era, required $5M+ revenue
- **Cook County Catalyst Grant** ($100,000) — applications closed Nov 2025; Lake County is outside the geography
- **UChicago Polsky / Microsoft / NVIDIA AI partnership** — university spinouts only
- **UW–Green Bay Investment Accelerator** ($100,000) — equity, food/beverage tech focus (poor fit)

_Research current as of September 18, 2026. Grant programs change — verify status on the official pages before applying._
